■ What we do

We make small programs that do one job and keep doing it. Most of them read something from the world (a sensor, a tide gauge, a notebook), keep it in a plain file, and show it on a plain web page. They run on cheap hardware, work offline, and need no account.

  measure     a sensor, a gauge, a person
      ↓
  keep        one plain file per day
      ↓
  show        one plain web page
